# Blender source of the arena

`ab-alone-scenes.blend` is the live Blender scene file of the art redesign (#1314, #1315), saved through the Blender MCP.

| Scene | What |
|---|---|
| `terrace` | The 1v1 arena, approved by the art director, and the Open world map |
| `kit_turntable` | The shared kit, each mesh on a turntable |
| `Scene` | The older `ow_*` Open world meshes |

`brief.md` and `coverage.md` are the art director's brief and the list of modes and bosses judged. `export/` holds the files the three.js port reads (see its `README.md`).
